Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Owesome ("we", "us") is an iPhone app where friends set tasks, share photo proof, and review each other. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password (stored securely and never visible to us), and the username and optional profile photo you choose.
  • Content you create: tasks, proof photos, comments, group chat messages and photos, punishment suggestions and votes, reactions, friend connections, and group settings such as backgrounds.
  • Reports and blocks: when you report content or block someone, we keep a record so we can review it and keep the community safe.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. We don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we don't show ads.

Camera and photos

Owesome asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to take or pick a photo (proof photos, chat photos, group backgrounds, or a profile picture). Photos are used only for that purpose. You can turn this access off any time in iPhone Settings.

How we use your information

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your tasks and chats to the friends you choose, and let friends review proof and vote.
  • To send you account emails, such as your sign-up code and password reset codes.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our rules, and suspending accounts that abuse the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Who can see your content

Your tasks, proof, and messages are visible to the friends and group members you share them with. Anyone you add as a friend or invite to a group can see what you post there. Please only add people you trust.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run Owesome: Supabase (database, login, and file storage) and an email service to deliver sign-up and password codes. These providers handle data only to provide their service to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from your Profile in the app. Deleting your account removes your account and the content you created. Some records related to safety reports may be kept for a limited time where needed to protect other users. You can also email us to request deletion.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls so people can only see what they're allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Owesome is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
