How it works

Three steps. No excuses, because your friends are watching.

1

Set a task

Pick something you want to get done, a deadline, and what happens if you miss it.

2

Show proof

Snap a photo before the deadline. Your friends review it and accept or decline.

3

Or owe the thing

Missed it? Friends suggest punishments and vote on the winner. Then you pay up.
