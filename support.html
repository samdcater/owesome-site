Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to your Profile, and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and your content. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we'll do it for you.

How do I report something?

On a task, tap the "Report this task" link below the review buttons. In a group, open the group menu and tap "Report this group". You can also email us with details. Reports are reviewed by a person.

How do I block someone?

Open the person's profile and choose Block. They won't be able to see your content or interact with you.

I didn't get my 8-digit sign-up code.

Check your spam or junk folder, then tap resend in the app. Codes expire after a short time, so use the newest one. Still nothing? Email us.

I forgot my password.

Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen and follow the steps. A code will be sent to your email.

Does Owesome send notifications?

Not yet. Open the app to see new tasks, reviews, and messages.

Safety

We don't allow harassment, hate, sexual content, or anything illegal. Content that breaks the rules is removed, and the people who post it can be removed from Owesome. See our Terms for details.
